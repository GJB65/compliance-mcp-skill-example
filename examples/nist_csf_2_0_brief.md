# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 compliance brief

**Jurisdiction**: United States  
**Version**: 2.0  
**Controls covered in this brief**: 10

## Framework summary

Voluntary framework for managing and reducing cybersecurity risk, organized around six core functions.

## Controls with auditor evidence

Each control below carries the structured evidence guidance auditors look for first. Use this as input to your agent's evaluation pass.

### GV.OV-01 - Risk management strategy outcomes are reviewed

**Evidence categories**: Strategy Oversight, KPI/KRI Reporting, Governance Cadence

**Artefacts an auditor will want:**
  - Strategy review meeting cadence and minutes
  - KPI/KRI dashboard tied to strategic objectives
  - Adjustments documented after strategy reviews
  - Annual cyber strategy refresh record
  - Action items from prior reviews and their closure status
  - Comparison of strategy targets vs. actual outcomes

**Common gaps:**
  - Strategy reviews are perfunctory with no resulting adjustments
  - KPIs defined but not reviewed at the strategic level
  - Action items raised in reviews never tracked to closure
  - Strategy updates not linked to outcome data
  - Review cadence absent or longer than annual

**Sources cited:**
  - NIST CSF 2.0 GV.OV-01 (NIST CSWP 29, Appendix A)
  - NIST SP 800-39 strategy oversight
  - ISO 27001:2022 Clause 9.3 management review

**Confidence**: 92/100

### GV.OV-02 - Risk management strategy is reviewed for coverage

**Evidence categories**: Strategy Coverage Review, Risk Universe Mapping, Coverage Gap Analysis

**Artefacts an auditor will want:**
  - Risk register cross-walk to strategy coverage areas
  - Annual strategy coverage review minutes
  - Documented strategy adjustments to close identified gaps
  - Mapping of strategy to enterprise risk taxonomy
  - Evidence of stakeholder input into the coverage review

**Common gaps:**
  - Strategy reviews focus on outcomes, not coverage breadth
  - Emerging risks not added to strategy on detection
  - Coverage gaps acknowledged but not closed
  - No formal cross-walk between risk register and strategy

**Sources cited:**
  - NIST CSF 2.0 GV.OV-02
  - NIST SP 800-39 strategy coverage

**Confidence**: 89/100
